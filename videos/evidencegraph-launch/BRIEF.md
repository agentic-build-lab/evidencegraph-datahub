## Intent

Create a first-place-caliber product demonstration for EvidenceGraph, an autonomous data-change assurance agent powered by open-source DataHub. The film must prove that DataHub is the operational context and authority layer, not a superficial metadata lookup: it changes the known blast radius from 3/7 to 7/7, supplies provenance for the plan, and removes the agent's authority when evidence is incomplete.
