## Intent

Create a first-place-caliber product demonstration for EvidenceGraph, an autonomous
data-change assurance agent powered by open-source DataHub. Sell the operational
value while showing real product evidence: a schema change, the complete impact
graph, generated migration artifacts, deterministic validation, fail-closed
behavior, verified writeback, idempotent retry, and a fresh-graph closure proof.
