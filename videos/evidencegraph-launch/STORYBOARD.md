## Video direction

Use the captured EvidenceGraph palette as a cinematic data-assurance system: deep ink canvas, warm light surfaces, aqua proof states, and orange only for unresolved risk. Keep Geist display, body, and mono roles large and decisive. Every frame reveals on the narrated cue using smooth long-tail motion, then settles; nothing front-loads in the first quarter. Frames 1 and 8 are deliberate held reads, while Frames 2 through 7 carry the analytical momentum. Keep all consequential content above the caption band. Never use generic AI gradients, bouncy entrances, lazy breathing, browser chrome, slideshow timing, or independent screensaver motion. No unsupported metric, object, or product claim may appear.
